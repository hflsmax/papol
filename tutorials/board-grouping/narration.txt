Related cards can turn a busy board into a clear structure.
Hold Shift and select two or more cards.
Choose Make booklet for an ordered thread. Papol stacks it into a vertical reading path.
For ideas without a fixed order, select another set and choose Make collection.
Collections keep a flexible layout. Choose Auto-arrange for a tidy grid, or Freeform to arrange cards yourself.
Booklets are paths. Collections are clusters. Enjoy Papol.
