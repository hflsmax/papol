When reading a paper, a relevant figure may be too far from the text to see.
When that happens, use the clipping feature in the viewer to keep it nearby.
The result is a movable clip. Resize it to fit, then place it beside the text.
You can also send the clip to a board.
A pinned clip moves with the paper as you scroll.
Choose Free float and scroll. The clip stays in place.
Enjoy Papol.
