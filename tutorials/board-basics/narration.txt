A board gives your research room to think out loud.
From your nook, choose New board, give it a name, and create it.
Drag files straight onto the board: first the Utah teapot, then the Stanford bunny.
Double-click an empty spot and type a thought of your own.
Press Control-V to paste a webpage address. Papol turns the link into a card.
Paste a YouTube address the same way, and it becomes another card.
Resize and move the cards wherever they fit.
Images, links, files, and thoughts are all cards you can arrange. Enjoy Papol.
