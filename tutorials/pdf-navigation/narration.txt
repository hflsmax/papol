You can navigate a PDF in Papol two ways: with link history and anchors.
Link history retraces jumps made by links inside the paper.
Follow a link, then use Back and Forward to move between locations.
The left and right bracket keys do the same.
When you want to move between two parts of a paper, drop an anchor in each place.
The anchors appear in My anchors. Click either anchor to go there.
Click a page label to name an anchor.
Turn an anchor into a note by selecting add a note, writing your note, and saving it.
Enjoy Papol.
