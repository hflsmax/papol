Papol gives you two ways to make notes while you read.
First, choose Anchor, then click beside the exact place you want to remember.
Open My anchors and select Add a note.
Write your thought, then save it to the anchor.
Now click Back to Papol. On the paper page, the same note appears under Notes.
Anchors work best when a thought depends on one precise passage or diagram.
For the second method, highlight text to save a passage. For a figure, choose Clipper and draw a box around it.
Select the clip's Send button, add your note, choose a board, and send it to that board.
Open the board and drag the saved figure onto the canvas.
Boards gather evidence across papers for comparison.
Anchors keep context. Boards connect. Enjoy Papol.
