Add a paper to Papol from its PDF in the Library.
Drop it here, or click the upload area and choose the file.
Papol extracts the metadata. Review it before saving.
By default, every account has two shelves: one called Display, and the other called Personal. Each paper inherits the visibility of its shelf: Display is public; Personal is private.
A paper can have multiple private tags.
Summaries are private; thoughts and ratings public.
Then save the paper.
Open it, and choose Read.
Enjoy Papol.
