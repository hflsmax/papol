# Telescoping: Papol is building the best features for programming language researchers

*September 30, 2026*

Hello,

Papol exists to take the friction out of reading papers, and we are building
the best features for programming language researchers. Today we are
launching our most exciting one yet: telescoping.

If you work in programming languages, you know the ritual. A proof cites
T-App on page 8, the rule is defined on page 7, and you scroll back and forth
until you have it memorised. Telescoping ends the scrolling: every mention of
a named rule in a paper now leads to the rule itself.

There are three ways to use it.

**Hover.** Rest the pointer on a rule's name and the rule appears right under
the line you are reading. Move on and it is gone.

![Resting the pointer on T-Var, T-Abs and T-App in turn shows each rule under the line](https://papol.io/assets/letters/telescoping-hover.gif)

**Click.** Go to the rule's definition. Press `[` to return to where you were,
and `]` to go there again.

![Clicking T-App goes to its definition on page 7, then the bracket keys go back to page 8, to the rule again, and back](https://papol.io/assets/letters/telescoping-click.gif)

**⌘ Click.** Keep a clip of the rule beside
its name, so it stays with you as you read the proof. Drag clips to arrange
them.

![⌘ clicking T-Var, T-Abs and T-App keeps each rule as a clip, set side by side under the line](https://papol.io/assets/letters/telescoping-clip.gif)

Telescoping is live on papol.io today, and in the latest Papol for Mac.

— the Papol Team
