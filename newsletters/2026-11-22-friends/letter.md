# Friends on Papol: you decide who sees what

*November 22, 2026*

Hello,

Our new Friends feature gives you additional ability to control who can see
your notes on the papers.

**Friends.** Friends can read your digs on the papers you keep on a public
shelf. Papers on a private shelf, and everything you write on them, stay
completely private to you.

**Lounge.** You see new papers and boards your friends put on their public
shelves, with their thoughts, ratings and digs.
