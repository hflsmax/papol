# Friends on Papol: you decide who sees what

*November 22, 2026*

Hello,

We have been improving Papol and adding features nonstop. The newest is
friends, and you decide who sees what.

**You choose your friends.** Press the small person icon on someone's nook, or
press **Find** in the Lounge to look for them by name or email. A request to
you arrives in your Inbox and by email; choose **Not now** and the asker is
told nothing. Either of you can end the friendship with the same icon.

**The Lounge shows what your friends share.** You see the papers they put on
their public shelves, with their thoughts, ratings and digs. Friends can read
and post in your digs on papers you keep on a public shelf. Move a paper to a
private shelf and that closes at once.

**Friends never see your reading.** What you read, when and for how long is
not shown to them. Nobody else sees your list of friends.

**You choose whether you can be found.** Open the new **Preferences** tab on
your profile. Set *People looking for friends* to *Cannot find me* and Find no
longer shows you, though people can still ask from your nook. *My email on my
nook* shows or hides your email address.
