# Friends on Papol: you decide who sees what

*November 22, 2026*

Hello,

We have been improving Papol and adding features nonstop. If you haven't used
it recently, give it a try.

**Friends.** Friends can read and post in your digs on the papers you keep on
a public shelf. Papers on a private shelf, and everything you write on them,
stay completely private to you.

**Lounge.** You see the papers and boards your friends put on their public
shelves, with their thoughts, ratings and digs.
