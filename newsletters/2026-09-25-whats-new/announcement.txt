Subject: What's new in Papol: whole folders, citation trails, and a note about share links

Hello,

It's been a busy week for Papol. Here is what changed.

ADD A WHOLE FOLDER OF PAPERS
Every upload box now takes several PDFs at once, or a whole folder: drop them in, or click "Add a folder". You review the batch before anything is saved. Papol shows which papers are new, skips the ones already in your nook, and lets you pick one shelf and tags for all of them.

EVERY PLACE A PAPER CITES A WORK
Click a citation such as [18] and its card says how often the paper cites that work. The up and down arrows step through each place. "Back to page …", or the [ key, takes you back to where you started.

LINKS THAT LAND WHERE THEY POINT
A link to a figure or table zooms until it fills the window. A link to a section opens at its heading, and footnote marks go to their notes. Every jump leaves a Back button.

MY ACTIVITY
Your profile shows the time you spent reading, by day, week or month, and on which papers. Only you can see it.

BOARDS
Link cards come onto a board at one size. Webpage cards are named by the page's title and show a picture of the page.

SHARE LINKS: PLEASE READ
Share links are now short, like papol.io/s/KedS24Oq7W. A share link made before September 25 no longer works. To share a paper again, open it, choose Share, and send the new link.

PAPOL FOR MAC
The Mac app is at 0.5.5. If yours is too old, it will ask you to update. "Mac app" at the top of papol.io has the latest.
