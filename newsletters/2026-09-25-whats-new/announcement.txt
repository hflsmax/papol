Subject: What's new in Papol: intelligent link navigation, reverse citation, and more

Hello,

Six new things in Papol, most useful first.

1. INTELLIGENT LINK NAVIGATION
Click "Figure 4" and the figure fills your window. A section link opens at its heading, and a footnote mark goes to its note. "Back to page …" (or [) returns you.

2. REVERSE CITATION
Click a citation such as [18] to see every place the paper cites that work, and step through them with the up and down arrows.

3. FOLDER DROP
Drop a folder of PDFs on any upload box. Review them in one list, skip the ones you have, and file them all at once.

4. TINY LINKS
Share links are now short, like papol.io/s/KedS24Oq7W. Links made before September 25 no longer work: open the paper, choose Share, and send the new link.

5. PAPOL ON YOUR MAC
Papol for Mac 0.5.5 keeps your papers with you. Get it from "Mac app" at the top of papol.io.

6. READING LOG
My activity on your profile shows how long you read, and on which papers. Only you can see it.
