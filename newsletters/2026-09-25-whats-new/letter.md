# What's new in Papol

*September 25, 2026*

Hello,

Six new things in Papol, most useful first.

## 1. Intelligent link navigation

Elsewhere, clicking "Figure 4" drops you somewhere on the page, and you
scroll and zoom to find it. In Papol, one click puts it right in front of
you: the figure fills your window, a section opens at its heading at a size
you can read, and tables, footnotes and algorithms arrive the same way.
**Back to page …** (or `[`) takes you back to where you were.

![Following a link to Fig. 4: the figure fills the window, with "Back to page 20" at the bottom](img/figure-link.png)

## 2. Reverse citation

Click a citation such as **[18]** to see every place the paper cites that
work, and step through them with ↑ ↓. You see how the authors use a source
without searching for it, and `[` brings you back.

![A citation card exploring the third of four places a work is cited, on page 7](img/citation-card.png)

## 3. Folder Drop

Your AI agent's literature review now comes into Papol in one drop. Click
**Add a folder**, give your agent the one-line prompt, and drop the folder it
makes. Papol opens the whole review in the agent's order, with the agent's
reason for including each paper kept as your private note. Papers it couldn't
get are listed for you to find, and papers you already have are skipped. Pick
a shelf, and the review is in your library.

![Reviewing the agent's folder "Transformers review": one new paper with its note, one missing, one behind a paywall, one already in the nook](img/folder-review.png)

## 4. Tiny links

Share links are now short, like `papol.io/s/KedS24Oq7W`, and show your
annotations as yours. Links made before September 25 no longer work: open
the paper, choose **Share**, and send the new link.

## 5. Papol on your Mac

Papol for Mac 0.5.5 keeps your papers with you, and opens any paper straight
from papol.io. Get it from **Mac app** at the top of papol.io; an older app
will ask you to update.

## 6. Reading log

**My activity** on your profile shows how long you read, by day, week or
month, and on which papers. Only you can see it.

![My activity: 7 h 46 min reading 6 papers this week](img/activity.png)
