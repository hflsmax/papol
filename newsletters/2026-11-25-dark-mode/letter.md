# Dark mode for your papers

*November 25, 2026*

Hello,

**Dark mode.** You can now read a paper as light text on a dark
background. Open a paper, press the gear in the top bar, and turn on Dark
mode under Pages; on a phone, go to Preferences and set PDF pages to Dark.
Your ink, anchors and clips change colour to stay easy to see on the dark
pages. You choose it separately on each device.
